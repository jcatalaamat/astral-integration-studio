X — Common Questions

things people ask.

answers to the questions that usually come up in the first email. if yours isn't here, it's the kind of thing we can discuss on a call.

Why not just hire a freelance web developer?+

you can. but a freelancer builds what you spec. which means you already need to know what you need — the architecture, the integrations, the flows. most practitioners don't, because that's not their work. i don't build from a spec. i learn your practice, make the architectural decisions, and stay to adjust them as your work evolves.

Why not just use Squarespace, Wix, or Kajabi?+

those tools are excellent if your practice fits their template. most don't. squarespace can't handle a five-level certification progression. kajabi can't host a retreat with intake + integration + homestay booking. wix can't do bilingual with real translation instead of google translate. the limitation isn't "is this a website" — it's "can this platform hold the shape of my actual work." usually it can't.

What if I just want a simple site for now?+

then you get a simple site for now. i build to the shape of your work, not to a fixed package. we can start with a clean presence and a booking flow, and add courses, membership, and automations as your practice grows. you're not signing up for a skyscraper on day one.

What do I own after the build?+

everything. the domain, the code repository, the database, the email list, the hosting accounts — every credential. i hand over ownership from the first commit. if you ever want to leave and take everything with you, you can. no lock-in, no held-hostage logins, no "our system only works with us."

What does the monthly fee cover?+

hosting, maintenance, security updates, performance monitoring, small iterations (copy changes, new offerings, new pages), and unlimited support. more importantly: my continued attention. when something needs to grow, someone who built it is watching.

Do you work with clients outside Mexico?+

most of the portfolio is. clients across mexico, the united states, europe, israel, india, and dubai. time zones don't matter when the build is async and the check-ins are weekly. what matters is whether your work fits the studio — honest, original, and made for real people.

How are you this fast?+

ten years of production engineering background, plus a custom AI-accelerated build system i've developed over the last two years. eight specialized agents handle scaffolding, copy, design, SEO, QA, and iteration in parallel. i stay the architect and the editor. what used to take a team of five, two months, now takes one person, one week. this is also why my rate makes any sense.

What if the build isn't right?+

we iterate until it is. the first build is a draft, not a deliverable. you click around, you tell me what feels wrong, and i adjust. i'd rather redo a section three times than hand over something that doesn't match your work.

Do you do one-off projects?+

occasionally, but the studio is built around ongoing partnership. a one-off site is a snapshot of a moment. your practice isn't a snapshot — it grows, changes, and needs someone who understands both the old shape and the new one. the monthly partnership is where the real value lives.

What if I'm not sure what I need?+

then send me whatever you have. a voice memo, a messy google doc, a screenshot of your current site, your instagram bio, a few sentences about what's frustrating you. that's enough. the first build is where "what do i need" gets answered, not the other way around.
