Dear friend,

If you've landed here you've probably already been around. You've clicked through agency sites that talk about digital transformation, freelancer portfolios with stock photography, and platform sales pages promising that their $149-a-month plan will finally solve it all. I won't do that to you. This is a letter, not a sales page.

I'm Jordi. I live in Mazunte — a small village on the Pacific coast of Oaxaca. I've been building websites and digital infrastructure for practitioners — yoga schools, reiki academies, retreat centers, healers, teachers doing original work — for about eleven years. Around 170 builds at this point, though I stopped counting carefully at 150.

I want to tell you what I do, why I do it this way, and why the whole thing costs what it costs.

One

What I do is straightforward: I build one platform that replaces seven tools. Your website. Your bookings. Your courses. Your member portal. Your email. Your community. Your payments. All of it on one domain, wired together from the first commit, yours to own from day one.

I build it in 48 hours.

I know that sounds like marketing. It isn't — it's the actual measurement for the actual first ship-ready build. You send me a voice memo, a messy Google doc, three screenshots of your current site, maybe a Linktree you're embarrassed of. Forty-eight hours later I send you a link. The copy is your copy, the photos are your photos, the bookings actually book, the payments actually pay. If it's wrong, we iterate. If it's right, we ship.

You can click through any of those if you'd like to see the work, though you don't need to read the rest of this letter through a portfolio's lens.

Three

Why this way — one person, one platform, from a hammock?

Because the alternative is the thing you already have. Seven tools you don't own. Five logins. Three dashboards that don't talk. One that takes 7% of every ticket. Monthly fees that compound. Digital infrastructure that feels like renting a storefront from a landlord who reads your mail.

The agencies will build you something custom but they won't stay. The freelancers will stay but they'll build on WordPress. The platforms will give you templates but they own everything you make. I'm the last technical hire you'll ever need — the one who stays, hands you the keys on day one, and maintains the whole thing for as long as your practice lasts.

The economic possibility of this comes from AI. Not the lazy copy-paste-hallucinate kind; the disciplined production-engineering kind. A decade of shipping production systems plus an AI copilot moves about five times faster than a decade of shipping production systems alone. That's why I can charge less than an agency for more than an agency would ship.

Five · the part I mean

Here's the part that doesn't go in pitches:

I think what you're doing matters. I don't mean that as a compliment — I mean it as a description. The people who come to you, for reiki or breathwork or a teacher training or six days in a retreat center, are looking for something their doctor and their therapist and their yoga app can't give them. Presence. Attention. The transmission of a lineage that isn't available on Spotify. You are doing some of the most serious work I know how to respect.

That's the work. That's what I build.
