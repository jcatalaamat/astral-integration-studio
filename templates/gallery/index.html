Case · 01 / 2025 · Featured

Devotional music,
unmediated.

A full discography, world-touring concert platform, and private members' community for two artists whose work used to live, in pieces, across five rented services.

Deva Premal and Miten came to me with 30 years of devotional music distributed across Eventbrite, Bandcamp, a Mailchimp list, a WordPress site, and an Instagram that bore all the weight. Every ticket sold was costing them 7% to a platform they didn't own. Every fan who signed up landed in three different databases that didn't speak.

In 14 days we consolidated everything under one domain — horizontal-scrolling album discography, tour pages that handle multi-currency ticketing across ten countries, a private listening room for members, and welcome automations narrated in Deva's own voice. Zero platform fees. One login.

Case · 02 / 2025

Twenty years of
ceremony, one home.

A ceremony center operating across five revenue streams on five disconnected platforms — unified into one multi-tenant platform in twelve days.

Retreats, teacher trainings, a members' circle, a small publishing line, private sessions. Each one had grown its own duct-taped payment flow, its own mailing list, its own scheduling tool. The founder had stopped expanding because the infrastructure couldn't hold another shape.

After our build, her new 1:1 mentorship program launched three weeks after the site went live — a thing that, by her own estimate, would have taken another six months of coordination on the old stack. Paid for itself in six months.

Case · 03 / 2025

Fifty years of teaching,
one domain.

A legendary Kundalini teacher with seven booking platforms serving classes across four cities consolidated into a single editorial landing and unified booking system.

When a teacher has taught continuously for fifty years, their students don't find them through a website — but the absence of a cohesive digital home becomes a kind of noise. Before our build: seven booking platforms, three newsletter tools, no unified schedule anyone could see. Eight days later it all sat in one place, in her voice, with a single calendar her students could actually use.

Case · 04 / 2024

A reiki academy with
an AI assistant.

Hundreds of students · multiple cert levels · practice-hour tracking · auto-generated practitioner directory · an AI trained on the methodology itself.

Shiv Energetics had outgrown every tool they'd tried. Five disconnected platforms, a spreadsheet serving as the practitioner directory, an inbox of students asking the same methodology questions. We rebuilt the whole operation in 23 days: student progression dashboards, certification tracking, practice-hour logging, an auto-updating directory, and a private AI assistant trained on 800 hours of the school's recorded teachings.

Case · 06 / 2024

Eight ticketing platforms,
one voice.

A vocal facilitator running events in eleven countries had their ticketing spread across eight services. Bilingual EN/HE members' portal built into a single platform in 11 days.
