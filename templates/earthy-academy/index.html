01

We keep the lineage undiluted.

Reiki is the transfer of source energy through a trained and attuned channel — nothing more, nothing less.

Today many modalities are sold as "Reiki" but are not a practical transfer of source energy. These branches confuse Reiki with religious belief, kundalini technique, breathwork, sound healing, plant medicine and more — mixtures that obscure what Reiki actually is.

ShivEnergetics™ is a trademarked academy dedicated to the Usui Shiki Ryoho method in its original form. The full transmission is twelve attunements — four at each of Reiki 1, 2 and 3 — passed face-to-face, from teacher to student, as Reiki has always been.

Additional skills — Pendulum, Crystal Healing, Psychometry, DPA Meditation, the Energetic Map Shifting Modality — are taught after the attunements, as tools for the practitioner's own development and professional competency. They are never used to activate the Reiki energy.

We hold this line so that the students of our students, and theirs after them, can trust what they are learning is the real thing.

From Manchester to Bangkok to Shanghai — and, finally, to the olive groves of Andalusia.

Sera is from Manchester, UK. After graduating from Leeds University in Sport, Health & Nutrition, she spent five years in Bangkok running her own personal training practice and began studying the body's energy system through Traditional Thai Massage and Traditional Thai Doula work at Wat Po.

In 2012 she found Reiki. She worked through the levels as every student does — through her own spiritual transformation — and became a teacher in 2014. For the next fourteen years in Shanghai she built her Reiki Academy: training medical professionals, yoga teachers, expat mothers, and a community that now continues her lineage in countries across the globe.

After nineteen years of city life, she exchanged the concrete for olive trees and the quiet of rural Granada, where she now hosts retreats, takes 1-1 students, and travels back to Asia for intensives. She is a Reiki Master Teacher at Level 9, and the founder of the Energetic Map Shifting Modality™.
