· How I work ·

Every woman arrives
with her own rhythm.

The work rests on three pillars — present in every session, in every conversation.
i.

Embodied presence

We work with the body — breath, sensation, and the nervous system — not only conversation. Insight becomes real when it is felt and lived.

ii.

Honest reflection

I offer clear, respectful mirrors. I name patterns, ask precise questions, and support truth-telling without judgment.

iii.

Integration

What arises in sessions is brought into daily life through practical steps and gentle integration, so change is lived over time.

· The work itself ·

What a session entails.

Each session is shaped by what you bring in that moment. Sessions are relational, responsive, and led by what is alive for you.

  • ❖Deep exploration and integration
  • ❖Emotional and energetic clearing
  • ❖Clarifying inner conflicts or recurring patterns
  • ❖Meeting and integrating different aspects of yourself
  • ❖Grounded guidance and reflection
  • ❖Practical steps for daily life
  • ❖Nervous-system-aware pacing and presence

The work moves at the pace of what is truly ready. Nothing is rushed. Nothing is forced.

· What I hold ·

I hold you
through.

Seven thresholds. Each one met with the same attentive presence — shaped to what you bring.
i.
Life transitions
Breakups, loss, awakenings, major changes. Becoming someone new while honoring who you've been.
ii.
Psychedelic integration
Medicine ceremonies, challenging experiences, self-led journeys. What the journey opened, brought gently back into the body.
iii.
Relationships
Intimacy, boundaries, shadow work, sacred partnership. The dance with the other as a mirror of the self.
iv.
Purpose & direction
Essence, gifts, vision, and path. The quiet call that has been waiting to be answered.
v.
Trauma & heartbreak
Meaning-making, gentle repair, repatterning. What hurt you met with care — so it can become something else.
vi.
Spiritual inquiry
Connection to source, ritual, remembrance. The practice of returning to yourself again and again.
vii.
Creative flow
Reconnecting with joy, trust, and expression. The part of you that knows how to make without asking permission.

Whatever the threshold — we begin with a conversation.

· About Nina ·

A steady hand at
the threshold.

I have been guiding this work for over ten years. I meet women heart to heart — with presence, honesty, and respect for each woman's rhythm and capacity.

My approach weaves embodied awareness, intuitive insight, and grounded integration. I work relationally and slowly, because depth cannot be forced.

I'm based between Mexico and Europe, and I work online with women across time zones. Sessions are private, confidential, and held in the language you dream in.
