About the builder.

JORDI AMAT — senior full-stack engineer, former startup CTO, shipping custom digital infrastructure from Mazunte, Oaxaca since 2013. Moved to a small village on the Pacific coast of Mexico after a decade of startups and began building for the people I consider to be doing the most original and under-supported work in the world: healers, teachers, retreat-holders, ceremony-keepers, and people who inherit lineages that aren't available on Spotify.

The economic logic of this studio rests on three things: a decade of shipping production systems, a disciplined use of AI tooling, and the absence of an agency overhead. The material logic rests on one: I live inside the world my clients do. Breathwork circles, cacao ceremonies, indigenous Oaxacan traditions, early mornings with coffee, late nights with a terminal. I don't build for this world from the outside.
