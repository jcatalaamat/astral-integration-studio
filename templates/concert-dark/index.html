The Story
Her father chanted the Gayatri Mantra to her in the womb. She chanted it to him as he took his last breath. The circle was complete.

Deva Premal grew up in a home of Sanskrit mantras and classical music in Nuremberg, Germany. At 11, she became a disciple of Osho. At 17, she traveled to India and found her path.

Miten was a London musician who opened for Fleetwood Mac and The Kinks. At 29, he left everything for an ashram in India. There he found meditation — and eventually, Deva.

Together they recorded their first album, The Essence, in the apartment where Deva was born. That album launched a global mantra revolution. 25+ albums, Grammy nomination, concerts from Siberia to Peru, and a community that spans every continent.

“The Gayatri Mantra was chanted to me before I was born. It is not something I learned. It is something I remembered.”
— Deva Premal
