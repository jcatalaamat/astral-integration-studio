§ About · Mariana Harpreet

From Mexico City to Mallorca — a life built in service of awakening.

“I am convinced that mental, emotional and spiritual health must be integral — body, mind and soul held as one.”

I was born in Mexico City and raised with an early, stubborn interest in the architecture of the mind, the body and the spirit — and a quiet certainty that any pain can be composted into a life of joy.

I hold a Bachelor of Arts, two Master's degrees in Gestalt and Systemic Psychotherapy, a Doctorate in Literature on the relationship between language and identity, and certifications as a Family Constellation Facilitator, Craniosacral Therapist and Kundalini Yoga Teacher.

Today I live in Palma de Mallorca, where I founded Sadhana Works — a conscious lifestyle studio I grew into one of the Mediterranean's most respected wellness companies. I serve as Regional Ambassador of the Chopra Foundation in Spain, and as President of the Wellness Tourism Association of Mallorca, where I work to turn the island into a world destination of consciousness.

I am a mother of three, which remains the teaching that contains all others.
