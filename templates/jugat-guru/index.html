· A note from the teacher ·

The practice I teach.

Kundalini is not a belief — it is a technology. A precise method for clearing the channels of the body so the soul can move through them. I teach it the way it was given to me: without modification, in the lineage of Yogi Bhajan, in the first hour before dawn. In the studio or in your pocket.

For eleven years I have asked one question of every student: will you sit for forty days? Everything else — the kriyas, the mantras, the Himalayan residencies, the private 1:1s — exists only to make that forty days possible. The practice is the teacher. I am only the room in which it is held.

This is not wellness content. This is discipline wrapped in beauty, and beauty wrapped in discipline. If that suits you, welcome. If not, there are easier teachers. I have no quarrel with them.

— Jugat Guru Dubai · April 2026
