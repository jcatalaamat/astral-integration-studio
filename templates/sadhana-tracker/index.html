I.
WHY FORTY DAYS

The number
is not
arbitrary.

In the yogic tradition, forty days is the minimum to break a habit. Ninety days to establish a new one. One hundred and twenty to confirm it. One thousand to master it.

II.
HOW IT WORKS

Four steps. Forty mornings. One self.

Step 01

Choose your kriya

Browse thirty-plus traditional practices. Filter by intention — clarity, release, devotion, prosperity, protection. Read the lineage. Pick one.

Step 02

Commit to forty

Block the time. Amrit Vela if you can — the hours before sunrise when the channels are open. Set your intention. Begin.

Step 03

Show up at dawn

Guided audio, mantra cues, silent timer, posture reminders. Low-light interface designed not to pull you out of trance. Offline-ready.

Step 04

Watch the arc

Daily reflection prompts. Unsent journal. A mandala of your forty days that fills with each sunrise. Insights surface on days seven, twenty-one, forty.
