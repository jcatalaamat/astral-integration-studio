▲ Featured · Q1 2026 Mar 2026 · JAMA Psychiatry

Kirtan Kriya and hippocampal plasticity: an 8-week randomised trial in early-stage cognitive decline

A single-blind RCT (n=182) compared a 12-minute daily Kirtan Kriya protocol against a matched music-listening control in adults with subjective cognitive decline. Hippocampal volume (MRI), verbal recall (RAVLT), and cortisol AUC were measured at baseline, week 8, and six-month follow-up. The Kirtan Kriya arm showed significant right-hippocampal thickening and sustained improvement in delayed recall — replicating the 2013 Khalsa et al. pilot at scale.

KHALSA D.S., NEWBERG A., KAUR G. · UCLA SEMEL INSTITUTE ▾ PDF  ·  DOI 10.1001/jamapsych.2026.0117
