I.  ·  Prologue

The healer begins as the healed.

Skylar had a spiritual awakening at nineteen and began holding healing circles at twenty. By her early thirties she had reversed her own autoimmune thyroid disease, chronic infections, and a decade of quiet self-neglect — not through pharmaceuticals, but through something older, harder to explain, and, she would come to argue, more accurate.

From that recovery she built a method. She calls it the Medical Intuitive Technique: a disciplined practice of reading the body as a transcript of belief. Of locating, in the architecture of a person's life, the small decision that made the illness reasonable.

She now works with a quiet roster of clients — mothers, founders, doctors, public figures, and a growing lineage of practitioners she certifies each autumn. Appointments are taken privately.

II.  ·  The Method

The Medical Intuitive Technique

A four-movement practice for locating the origin of illness — the place where belief becomes biology.

I.
The Scan

Every illness leaves a signature in the body's energetic field. The first act of the work is to locate it — thought, emotion, belief, inherited pattern.

II.
The Cause

Behind every persistent symptom is a belief the body has organised itself around. The symptom is reasonable once the belief is seen.

III.
The Clearing

The body will release what the mind consents to release. Clearing is done at the level of the belief, not the level of the organ.

IV.
The Integration

Healing is the body's return to original blueprint — coherence restored between thought, emotion, and tissue. It is quieter than people expect.
