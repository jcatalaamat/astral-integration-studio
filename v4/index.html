I — The Situation

you changed someone's life this week.

and then spent the weekend fighting Kajabi.

your practice runs on seven platforms that don't speak to each other. each one sends you a bill. each one holds a piece of your work hostage. students who want to sign up get sent through three different tools.

you're using their platforms to build their platforms with your content. and your digital presence doesn't match the depth of your teaching — not even close.

II — The Alternative

one home. everything connected.

stop duct-taping seven platforms together. get one that was built for you, owned by you, and evolves with your teaching.

VII — Common Questions

things people ask.

answers to the questions that usually come up in the first email. if yours isn't here, it's the kind of thing we can discuss on a call.

Why not just hire a freelance web developer?+

you can. but a freelancer builds what you spec. which means you already need to know what you need. i don't build from a spec. i learn your practice, make the architectural decisions, and stay to adjust them as your work evolves.

Why not just use Squarespace, Wix, or Kajabi?+

those tools are excellent if your practice fits their template. most don't. squarespace can't handle a five-level certification progression. kajabi can't host a retreat with intake + integration + homestay booking. the limitation isn't "is this a website" — it's "can this platform hold the shape of my actual work."

What if I just want a simple site for now?+

then you get a simple site for now. we can start with a clean presence and a booking flow, and add courses, membership, and automations as your practice grows. you're not signing up for a skyscraper on day one.

What do I own after the build?+

everything. the domain, the code repository, the database, the email list, the hosting accounts — every credential. if you ever want to leave and take everything with you, you can. no lock-in, no held-hostage logins.

What does the monthly fee cover?+

hosting, maintenance, security updates, performance monitoring, small iterations, and unlimited support. more importantly: my continued attention. when something needs to grow, someone who built it is watching.

Do you work with clients outside Mexico?+

most of the portfolio is. clients across mexico, the united states, europe, israel, india, and dubai. what matters is whether your work fits the studio — honest, original, and made for real people.

How are you this fast?+

ten years of production engineering plus a custom AI-accelerated build system. eight specialized agents handle scaffolding, copy, design, SEO, QA, and iteration in parallel. what used to take a team of five, two months, now takes one person, one week.

What if the build isn't right?+

we iterate until it is. the first build is a draft, not a deliverable. you click around, you tell me what feels wrong, and i adjust. i'd rather redo a section three times than hand over something that doesn't match.

Do you do one-off projects?+

occasionally, but the studio is built around ongoing partnership. your practice isn't a snapshot — it grows and changes. the monthly partnership is where the real value lives.

What if I'm not sure what I need?+

then send me whatever you have. a voice memo, a messy google doc, a screenshot, your instagram bio. that's enough. the first build is where "what do i need" gets answered.
