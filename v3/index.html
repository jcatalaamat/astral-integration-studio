/ One person · one platform · no duct tape

I'm Jordi. I build the quiet machinery behind sacred work — websites, bookings, courses, payments, community, everything — for healers, teachers, retreat centers, and people doing original work in the world.

$250 intake fee — credited toward your build if we work together.

Your practice runs on seven platforms that don't speak to each other. Each one sends you a bill. Each one holds a piece of your work hostage. Students who want to sign up for your class get sent through three different tools. Your intake is a Google Form. Your community is scattered across a WhatsApp group, a Substack, and an Instagram DM thread you can't search.

You're using their platforms to build their platforms with your content. And your digital presence doesn't match the depth of your teaching — not even close.

I do the opposite. I build one platform. I own it with you. You own it alone. It stays as long as your practice lasts.

/ About the builder

The builder,
briefly.

Senior full-stack engineer. Former CTO. I spent a decade building production systems and leading engineering teams in startups you've probably heard of. Then I moved to Mazunte, Oaxaca — a small village on the Pacific coast of Mexico — and started building for the people doing what I consider the most original and under-served work in the world: education, healing, ceremony, community.

I live inside the same world my clients do. Breathwork circles, cacao ceremonies, indigenous Oaxacan traditions, plant medicine, sound, silence, early mornings with coffee and a journal, late nights with a terminal. I don't build for this world from the outside. I build from inside it.

The technical competence is old — a decade of shipping production systems — but the speed is new. I work with AI tools that let me move at a velocity that used to require a full team. And I charge a fraction of what an agency would because my overhead is a hammock, a satellite dish, and a cup of Oaxacan coffee.

I don't do sales calls. I don't send proposals. I find people whose work I respect, I build them something real, and I send them the link. If they like it, we work together. If not, they keep the build anyway.

They were never separate processes. Building and healing are the same service — listening on different ports.

§08 / Notices / Common questions · all visible No accordions

Things people ask.

All ten answers visible by default. No accordions, no expand-to-read. If yours isn't here, it's the kind of thing we can discuss on a call.

№ 01Why not just hire a freelance web developer?
Because a freelance web developer builds websites. You don't need a website — you need a platform. Seven tools replaced by one. I build production infrastructure and stay for the life of your practice.
№ 02Why not Squarespace or Kajabi?
You can. Many people do. They're fine if you're starting out with no audience. The problem shows up around month eighteen — when your practice has grown and the templates can't hold what you're teaching. That's when clients come to me.
№ 03What if I just want a simple site?
That's the Practitioner tier — $2,500 setup, $300/mo. A beautiful, custom, fast site with bookings and payments. You can grow into Studio or Complex later without migrating off the platform.
№ 04What do I own?
Everything. Every line of code, every database, every domain, every credential, your full email list, every student record. From the first commit. If you ever want to leave, I hand over the keys — takes one afternoon.
№ 05What does the monthly fee cover?
Hosting, domain, SSL, security updates, backups, uptime monitoring, AI infrastructure costs, minor content updates, bug fixes, and me — your technical partner on call. New features scoped separately at my rate, not agency rate.
№ 06Clients outside Mexico?
Mostly, actually. Clients in 17 countries. The work is async-first — no timezone problems. Two or three live calls per month per client, scheduled around your morning practice, not mine.
№ 07How are you this fast?
Ten years of production engineering + AI-native workflows + no sales or project-management overhead. I don't write proposals. I don't do discovery sprints. I just build. Eight hours of deep work with AI moves more than five agency people can in a week.
№ 08What if the build isn't right?
We iterate. First build in 48 hours, then up to two rounds of major revisions at no extra cost. If after that it's still not right, we part ways — you keep the build. Has not happened in 170+ projects. Door is open.
№ 09Do you do one-off projects?
Rarely. The monthly partnership is how I keep rates low and quality high. One-offs cost roughly double because context-switching is real. If you want one-off work, I'll usually refer you.
№ 10What if I'm not sure what I need?
That's normal. Most clients come to me saying "I have a mess and I don't know what I need." Send me the mess. I'll tell you what I see. If it's a fit we start; if not I'll point you somewhere else. First conversation costs nothing.
